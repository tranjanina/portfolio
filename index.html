About Janina

A poet's ear. An analyst's eye. Always hungry.

I've lived in Hong Kong, Bali, and Singapore, immersed in the food, hotels, and cultural textures of all three. My literary foundation is a degree in Academic and Creative Writing with a concentration in Poetry and Analysis—giving me both a lyricist's ear for emotional cadence and an analyst's eye for rigorous narrative structure.

Before stepping into copywriting and hospitality creative direction two years ago, I spent six years in Hong Kong as Head of English and an admissions coach for overseas college and university applications, including at Telos Education Hong Kong. That career demanded crafting authentic, high-stakes personal narratives that stood out to the world's most selective institutions—a storytelling rigor that now directly powers how I position luxury dining rooms and boutique properties.

Today, my practice bridges culinary photography, brand development, and editorial press releases: creating lifestyle features for Sassy Mama Hong Kong, architecting launch releases for Wrong Gym Bali (featured in Tatler Singapore), and directing visual stories for luxury stays like La Réserve 1785 Bali. I don't treat hospitality as generic promotional content; I treat it as cultural, sensory, and emotional storytelling that captures what makes a table or property truly distinct.
